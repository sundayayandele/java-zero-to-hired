# Safe Exam Browser

← [Home](../../README.md) · [The self-study route](self-study-route.md)

**From 1 December 2026**, every end-of-phase exam and every timed coding test on itlearning.tech must be taken in **Safe Exam Browser (SEB)**, a free browser that locks your computer to the exam: no other apps, websites or AI assistants while it runs. Topic quizzes and the coding practice quiz don't need it.

## What you need

- A **Windows** or **macOS** computer (or an **iPad**) on which you can install software.
- **Safe Exam Browser**, free from [safeexambrowser.org/download_en.html](https://safeexambrowser.org/download_en.html): the Windows or macOS version, or *SEB* from the App Store on an iPad.
- A stable internet connection, and your charger: once you start, you can't switch to other apps.

SEB doesn't run on **Linux** or **Chromebooks**. If that's all you have, take the exam on a Windows or macOS computer you can borrow (a friend's, a work or school computer if you're allowed to install SEB, or a cyber café that lets you). There's no other way to take these exams.

## Before exam day

1. Install SEB and restart your computer.
2. **On a Mac, allow Full Disk Access first:** open **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access** and turn on **Safe Exam Browser** (if it isn't listed, click **+** and add it from Applications). SEB needs it to check for apps that aren't allowed during an exam. Without it, SEB starts in the background and waits on a message you may not see.
3. **Use one screen.** Unplug any second monitor before you start: SEB uses your computer's own screen, and its messages appear there.
4. **Try it first:** open the exam's page on itlearning.tech (you don't have to start an attempt) and click **Launch Safe Exam Browser**. SEB opens itlearning.tech; quit it again (it asks you to confirm). If it doesn't open, use **Download configuration** and open the downloaded file instead.
5. Close everything you don't need: SEB asks you to quit apps that aren't allowed during an exam, such as Zoom, Teams or screen-sharing tools.

## On exam day

1. Log in to itlearning.tech in your normal browser and open the exam.
2. Click **Launch Safe Exam Browser**. SEB opens, full screen, on the exam page; log in there if asked.
3. Take the exam. The timer keeps running if SEB closes, so don't quit until you've submitted.
4. When you've submitted, quit SEB (top bar or taskbar, then confirm).

## If something goes wrong

- **"You need Safe Exam Browser" in your normal browser:** that's expected; click **Launch Safe Exam Browser**. If your browser asks whether to open Safe Exam Browser, choose **Open**.
- **On a Mac, nothing happens when you launch SEB, or SEB "can't be moved to the Trash because it's open":** SEB is running in the background, waiting on a message about Full Disk Access. Quit it (press **Cmd + Option + Esc**, choose Safe Exam Browser, **Force Quit**), allow Full Disk Access as in *Before exam day* step 2, unplug any second monitor, and try again.
- **On a Mac, macOS says SEB can't be opened:** go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** next to Safe Exam Browser, and try again.
- **Your Touch Bar changed after quitting SEB:** set it back in **System Settings › Keyboard › Touch Bar shows**.
- **SEB says the configuration is wrong or the key doesn't match:** go back to the exam page and use **Download configuration**, then open that file.
- **Your computer crashes during the exam:** restart, open the exam again and launch SEB; your attempt continues where it was, with the time that's left.
- **Still stuck:** ask in your course's *Questions and help* forum before your exam day, with your operating system and a screenshot.
